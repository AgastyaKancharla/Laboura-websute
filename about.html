About Laboura: two corners of the same city.

People who
need work Businesses that
need staff

People in one part of the city were looking for work. Businesses in another part were looking for staff. They were close, and they never met. Laboura connects them.

What is Laboura?

noun

  1. 1A staffing and placement company in India that finds jobs near home for people who need work.
  2. 2The quick way for a local business to hire verified staff, in 6 to 15 days.
  3. 3A free missed call that ends in a job.

As in: "Need work near home? Call Laboura."

Why Laboura was started

Our co-founder kept seeing the same thing. People wanted work. Businesses wanted staff. They lived in the same city, and no one connected them. Laboura was started to be that connection, one call at a time.

Problems Laboura solves for job seekers and businesses

Two problems.
One answer.

People who need work and businesses that need staff face the same thing from two sides: no trusted way to find each other nearby. Here is what Laboura changes.

  1. If you need work

    Paying agents and getting nothing.

    Laboura: ₹499 once. No job offer in 30 days? Full refund. Nothing else, ever.

  2. If you need staff

    Staff leave in a month.

    Laboura: Leaves in 60 days? We replace free.

  3. If you need work

    Jobs too far from home.

    Laboura: We match jobs close to where you live.

  4. If you need staff

    No time to check strangers.

    Laboura: We check ID, address and past work.

  5. If you need work

    No CV, no degree, no chance.

    Laboura: No CV needed. Skills matter more than marks.

  6. If you need staff

    Weeks of calls to hire one person.

    Laboura: One call. Verified staff in 6 to 15 days.
