Laboura questions and answers

Ask anything.
Simple answers.

Is Laboura free for job seekers?

Calling Laboura is free. To register, you pay a one-time fee of ₹499 + GST. If you do not get a job offer within 30 days, you get the full ₹499 back.

Why does Laboura charge ₹499? Is it genuine?

The ₹499 pays for a real person who talks to you, checks your profile and fixes your interviews with verified businesses. It is paid once, only through the Laboura app or our official number. No job offer in 30 days means a full refund. We never ask for a deposit, a training fee or a joining fee.

How do I get my refund?

If 30 days pass without a job offer, call Laboura or message us on WhatsApp. We send the full ₹499 back to you.

Do I need a CV or a degree?

No. Just tell us your skills on the phone. Many jobs need no degree and no CV.

I passed only 10th or 12th. Is there work for me?

Yes. Most jobs we fill ask for 10th pass, 12th pass or less. Your skills matter more than your marks.

I have no experience. Can I still get a job?

Yes. Many shops, hotels, warehouses and factories hire freshers as helpers and train them on the job.

Will the job be near my home?

Yes. We look for work close to where you live, so you spend less time and money on travel.

I need a job urgently. How fast can I start?

Tell us you can join right away. We look first at businesses that want people to start immediately.

Can I get a high-paying job without a degree?

Yes. Drivers, cooks, electricians, field sales staff and skilled factory workers often earn more. Tell us your skills and experience, and we look for the best pay near you.

How much salary will I get?

It depends on the job, your experience and your city. We tell you the salary before the interview, so there are no surprises.

Are there jobs for women?

Yes. Shops, hospitals, salons, hotels and offices hire women. Tell us the timings that are safe and easy for you, and we match jobs to them.

Are there part-time or night shift jobs?

Yes. Tell us the hours you can work: morning, evening, night or part-time. We match you to jobs with those timings.

Can I get a job with food and a place to stay?

Some hotels, factories and warehouses give food and a room. Tell us you need it, and we look for those jobs first.

I am over 40. Can I get a job?

Yes. Security, housekeeping, driving, store keeping and many other jobs hire people of every age.

I already have a job. Can I find a better one?

Yes. Register even while you are working. We look for a job with better pay or a shorter travel time.

Can I find a job for someone in my family?

Yes. You can call for your son, daughter, husband, wife or a friend. We talk to them before we fix any interview.

Can I talk in my own language?

Yes. Our team speaks the local language in every state we work in, plus Hindi and English.

What do I need to register?

A phone number and your Aadhaar card. Give a missed call to 1800-XXX-XXXX or download the Laboura app.

How fast can I get staff?

Most businesses meet checked candidates within 6 to 15 days of their call.

I need staff urgently. Can you help?

Yes. Tell us it is urgent. We send checked candidates who live close to your business first.

What does "verified" mean?

We check each person's ID, address and past work before we send them to you.

What if the person I hire leaves?

If a hire leaves within 60 days, Laboura sends a replacement free.

Will the staff stay with my business?

We match people by distance, skills and pay, so they are more likely to stay. If someone leaves within 60 days, we replace them free.

How much does Laboura charge a business?

Half of the first month's salary, plus GST, for each person you choose after the interview. For example, for a job that pays ₹10,000 a month, you pay ₹5,000 + GST. Every hire comes with a 60-day free replacement.

Do I pay if you can't fill the job?

No. You pay only after you interview someone and choose them.

Can I hire for many shops or branches?

Yes. One person at Laboura handles all your hiring, for one shop or many.

Can I hire a full team at once?

Yes, from one person to a full team. For a big team, fill our company enquiry form with every job and how many people.

Is Laboura a job portal?

No. Laboura is a staffing company. A real person talks to you, checks every profile and fixes the interview. You do not have to scroll through job ads.

Where does Laboura work?

In 8 states: Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh and Delhi. That includes Bengaluru, Chennai, Kochi, Hyderabad, Visakhapatnam, Mumbai, Pune, Lucknow and New Delhi.

Does Laboura find jobs abroad?

Not yet. Laboura Global, for verified Indian workers who want jobs abroad, is coming soon.
