Questions about salary

Salary answers.

What is the average salary for blue-collar jobs in India?

Reports put the average starting salary for blue-collar jobs at about ₹15,265 a month in 2025. Pay depends on the job, your experience and your city.

Which blue-collar jobs pay the most?

Field sales is among the highest-paying blue-collar work, and delivery and driver pay has been rising fastest. Skilled work like electrician or cook with a speciality also pays more than helper roles.
